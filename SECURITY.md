# Security policy

Quatm controls laboratory hardware. Please report suspected vulnerabilities
privately to **security@quatm.com**. Do not open a public issue for a
vulnerability.

Include the affected component/version, impact, reproduction steps, and
sanitised logs. The team aims to acknowledge reports within 24 hours and will
coordinate a fix and disclosure timeline with the reporter.

The public policy is also available at
<https://quatm.com/en/security-policy/>.
